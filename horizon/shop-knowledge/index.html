The shop, as Horizon sees it

Written from what it read, not from a form

Halden Metalworks is a fab shop. Most of the work is weldments and sheet metal in A36 and 304: frames, brackets, and enclosures in lots of 10 to 500. Setup drives the price on short runs; brake time drives it on long ones. Larkspur and Marlin Marine send most of the repeat work, and 61% of parts are repeats priced from the last job. Powder coat goes out to Summit. Anything in Inconel, or over $60,000, is quoted by hand.
