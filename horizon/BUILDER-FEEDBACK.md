# Builder-page feedback, transferred to Horizon (compiled Nov 4 2026)

Checklist of every piece of feedback Alex, Rob, Sunny, Rachel, Annika, Jess and Jon gave on the Builder page, hero demo,
promo video, AS9100 draft and nav rebuild (Sept–Nov 2026), used to audit the Horizon page and loops. Items marked
[builder-only] do not transfer literally. Sources: memory files, #marketing, the Alex/Rob/Annika/Carolyn group DM,
Alex's DM, Rachel's story threads, Linear F-1158/1125/1081/1082/1098/1099/1102/1155/1212/1231.

## 1. Copy & claims
- No "Archie" prefix on Builder or Horizon (Alex 10/6; Carolyn 10/20).
- "fulcrum" lowercase in display text; product names keep their capital (Alex 10/12; Carolyn 10/15).
- American spelling; grep `olour` before publishing (Carolyn 10/13).
- Never reporting/dashboard framing (Alex 10/12, 10/15, 10/23; Annika 10/27).
- Every example must read custom and impressive, never something Fulcrum already does (Alex 10/12, 10/20).
- Never phrase an example as making up for a Fulcrum gap (Alex 10/8; Annika 10/8).
- The customer must not sound like they did a lot of work; text → thing in minutes (Alex 10/23, 10/27).
- Don't claim "no software company would build it" (Annika 10/27).
- Don't lead with "something is slowing your shop down" as the only motivation (Alex 10/20).
- No growth/ROI/customer-count numbers without Sunny's written OK; "hundreds of customers" unsupported (Carolyn 10/7; F-1081).
- Don't name a count that makes adoption look small (Annika, Alex 10/27).
- No roadmap dates, no model-vendor names; keep cloud wording generic (F-1125; Carolyn 10/7).
- Only claim writes/actions the product actually does; verify every "it does X" with product (10/7 qa3; demo-video v13).
- Flag and confirm any unshipped capability shown in a demo (10/6).
- Never "on every plan" for a paid add-on (Carolyn 10/15).
- "Trial", never "beta", once launched (Alex 10/14, 10/27).
- Voice bans: ERP, em dashes, "automations" for unshipped runs, seamless / end-to-end / comprehensive / transforms / boosting throughput / modern technology; use contractions (10/7; 11/3).
- No "bolt-on AI" phrasing; say what it does (Rachel 10/22).
- Plain words a non-technical reader knows (Annika 10/27).
- Keep every example consistent with the central claim (Annika 10/27).
- Keep hitting the approved messaging lines in every asset (Alex 10/6, 10/27).
- Horizon: communication framing, never "estimating tool"; never "replace Paperless Parts"; no accuracy numbers; no "ERP agnostic" until built; not free; no Horizon in public headlines before the mid-Nov push.

## 2. Hierarchy & sections

- No 3-step "how it works" stepper section (Carolyn 10/7) [builder-only section, lesson transfers].

- Second half of a before/after beat shows the user inside the product, not in chat (Alex 10/8) [builder-only beat].
- Rethink section needs a genuinely impressive example (Alex 10/12).
- Customer section: caps eyebrow, name + logo + problem + what they built, click-driven carousel, distinct palette per card (Rob/Alex 10/7–10/9; Carolyn 10/8).
- Walkthrough video + resource links low on the page (10/7, open 10/14).
- FAQs as an accordion (Carolyn 10/6).
- Headlines wrap one sentence per line on desktop; statement band plain text, no highlight block (Carolyn 10/7).
- Horizon order (Alex 11/2): managing agents on an exception basis; profile → how you work → rules; Jon's scenarios as email → thinking → highlighted answer.
- Vertical pages lead with the vertical and the product connection, overtly (Alex 10/14).

## 3. Hero & demo
- No static screenshots; every product visual is a live loop (Carolyn 10/6).
- Hero in the same animation style as the live Archie/Builder page (Carolyn 10/7).
- Hero framed natively, centered, full width; never "shoved into the section" (Carolyn 10/7).
- No blank canvas while the demo loads: open on a finished state, poster + skeleton + status chip, dim-not-clear between loops (Carolyn 10/28–29; Jess's doc).
- Demo loads and runs quickly: no idle countdown, speed up slow beats (Carolyn 10/7, 10/28).
- Demo cycles through all examples (Alex 10/8).
- Demo uses the CURRENT product UI, labeled nav, no stale labels (Alex 10/12; Rob 10/13).
- Make clear it is simulated: read-only prompt + "Simulated on sample data" note (Carolyn 10/6).
- Every company, person, PO, part, operator is fictional and checked against Salesforce; never real primes/vendors (Carolyn 10/6, 10/8).
- Persona avatars from the brand-kit stock photos, not icons (Carolyn 10/7).
- Overlays that are not product screens sit opaque and OUTSIDE the app frame (Carolyn 10/14).
- Panels show values and text+dot statuses, not pills/badges/chips; make demos more visual (Carolyn 10/6).
- Built design fills the space; side chat only during the "change it" beat (Carolyn 10/14) [builder-only].
- Opening beat full screen, then the built thing (Carolyn 10/14) [builder-only].
- Typing readable in every version; nothing loads too fast (Carolyn 10/6, 10/13).
- No weird pacing in any beat (Alex 10/15).
- Approvals/publishing happen through the product UI, never inside chat (Alex 10/14).
- Horizon hero shows an Outlook inbox so the email visibly comes from another client (Carolyn 11/2).
- Static video opening animated like the Control video with a color field (Alex 10/12).

## 6. Visual & motion
- Highlights as brand-blue text, not blocks; if a background highlight is used, Rob's rule (line-height 1, padding, equal above/below, box-decoration-break) (Rob 10/1; Carolyn 10/6, 10/13).
- Brand kit: ink #292932 bands, lime #e8ff5e, Gilroy display / Inter body, 12px frames with float shadow (no border + shadow), Interaction Blue inside product frames, Brand Blue on marketing chrome, one neon per composition (10/6; 11/3).
- Headings centered via the site class; no team pills in the hero; no "1 of 4" badges (Carolyn 10/6).
- Equal icon-to-title and title-to-body gaps on feature cards (Carolyn 10/14).
- Type weights match the site: H1/feature 700, kicker 600, body 300 (Carolyn 10/13).
- No text cut off at 1440 / 1920 / tablet / 390; embeds width 100% with max-width (Carolyn 10/6).
- Demo loads and fits on phones (Carolyn 10/29).
- prefers-reduced-motion shows a static finished state.
- Gate loops on in-view; keep media small (10/7).
- No wordmarks or logos inside animations; no texture video or gradient blobs behind the demo (Carolyn 10/7).
- Video covers carry a brand color field; presenter cut out; laptop shut (Alex 10/12; 10/13).
- Smooth over choppy, action after the ask, no numbered badges on cards (Rob 10/6; Carolyn 10/7).
- Real licensed music cut to downbeats; never generated beds or per-card SFX (Carolyn 10/14).
- Announcement treatments: self-contained blue banner with blue button; grey reads disabled; no presenter photo overload (Rob 10/14; Alex 10/14).

## 7. Process
- One owner per Webflow page at a time (Carolyn 10/6).
- Draft/noindex handling: a full-site publish can leak a draft:false page; redrafting after a staging publish can 404 it (10/6).
- One change → one publish; minute estimates; first visible result fast (Carolyn 10/14).
- Nothing ships until Alex signs off on the landing page (Alex 10/8).
- All finals in ONE review artifact with approve/needs toggles; pass Carolyn → Jess/Rachel → Alex (Alex 10/15).
- Launch all channels at once at the best send time (Alex 10/15).
- Segment drips by engagement cohort; value first, loss aversion last (Alex 10/6, 10/27).
- Re-brief contractors on positioning before they draft (Alex 10/26).
- Launch plan in the Control-doc structure (Carolyn 10/1).
- Mark AI-drafted copy as drafts; leave Carolyn's edits alone (10/7).
- Vertical pages that look bad stay out of the nav until approved (Carolyn 10/13).
- Before publishing a duplicate, clear the source page's CSS/JS embeds and bump ?v= on every iframe.
